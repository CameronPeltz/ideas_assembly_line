# Floor Schedule to iCal & Enhanced Congressional Schedule Aggregation

Transform floor schedules into iCal and integrate with committee schedules for a unified congressional calendar.

## The problem

Floor schedules and committee schedules are typically provided in separate, non-integrated formats, making it difficult to get a holistic view of the congressional calendar.

## The proposed idea

from https://congressional-tech-backlog.netlify.app/#pII.4

Convert House/Senate floor schedules into .ics feeds, then integrate with committee schedules.

## Why this seems promising

The schedules seem all over the place on different websites so it would be easier for people in Congress to keep track and up to date on sessions/schedules.
