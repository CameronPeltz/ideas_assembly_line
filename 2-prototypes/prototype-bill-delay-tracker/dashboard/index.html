This page measures the introduced stage: CRS writes summaries for introduced versions only in the 119th Congress ("CRS is not writing bill summaries for subsequent versions of measures as they move through the legislative process", Congress.gov help, Bill Summaries). The – later-stage summaries CRS wrote anyway are left out here and kept in the data under their stage. The text date is the day GovInfo took the version in (the MODS record's dateIngested), not the date printed on the bill, which runs days to weeks earlier; the CSV exports carry both. The summary date is what the Congress.gov API documents as the date the summary was last updated; it exposes no first-publication date, so a summary revised later reads slower than it was.
